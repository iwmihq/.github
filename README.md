## About IWMI

[IWMI](https://www.iwmi.cgiar.org) is an international research-for-development organization, with offices in 15 countries and a global network of scientists operating in more than 55 countries. For over three decades, our research has led to changes in water management that contribute to social and economic development.

Our vision is a water-secure world. We work on the water and land management challenges faced by poor communities in developing countries, contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals on poverty, hunger and a sustainable environment.

## What you will find here

This is the official home for IWMI's code. Repositories here include:

- **Research code and models**: analysis workflows, water accounting, hydrology and earth observation tools
- **Digital platforms**: the applications, APIs and data pipelines behind IWMI's decision-support tools

The pinned repositories below are a good place to start.
